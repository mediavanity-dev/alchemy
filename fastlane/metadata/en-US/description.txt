Sing, hum or play a melody, and Alchemy finds the key it sits in and the chords that fit underneath it. It's the fastest way to turn the tune in your head into a song you can play.

HUM IT, SEE IT
• Your pitch draws live as you sing, on a scrolling note grid
• Alchemy picks out every note, even through vibrato and slides
• Works with your voice or any instrument: guitar, piano, violin, whistling

FIND THE KEY
• Detects major and minor keys, plus Dorian and Mixolydian modes
• Shows how likely each key is, so you can try the alternatives

GET A CHORD PROGRESSION
• Suggests chords that fit your melody and move like a real song
• Choose triads, extended chords (7ths, sus, add9) or chromatic colour with borrowed chords
• Change chords every 2 bars, every bar, half bar or beat
• Switch between several progressions that fit, in the same key
• Tap any chord to hear ranked alternatives

HEAR IT YOUR WAY
• Six chord sounds: warm pad, electric piano, guitar, organ, strings and marimba
• Five playing styles: held, on the beat, strummed, arpeggio, or bass & chord

BUILD A SONG
• Record extra layers: harmonies, instruments or beats, with a count-in
• Loop recording keeps every pass so you can pick your best
• Lock your melody to the beat and gently tune your voice
• A metronome follows your tempo

TAKE IT ANYWHERE
• Export melody, chords and bass as a MIDI file for GarageBand, Logic or any DAW
• Share a chord chart as text
• Import audio files, or send Voice Memos straight to Alchemy from the Share menu

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything happens on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your recordings never leave your device.

Perfect for songwriters, singers, music students and anyone who has ever had a tune stuck in their head.
