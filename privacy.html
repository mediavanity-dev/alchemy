Alchemy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

The short version

The Alchemy iPhone app does not collect, store on a server, or share any of your data. Everything you record stays on your device.

Microphone

Alchemy uses the microphone only while you are recording, so it can find the notes, key and chords of what you sing or play. The audio is analysed on your iPhone and is never uploaded.

Your recordings and library

Takes you save are stored only in the app's storage on your device. They are deleted if you delete them in the Library or remove the app. They are shared only when you choose to share a chord chart or MIDI file through the iOS share sheet.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The iPhone app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and does not track you across apps or websites.

The separate web version of Alchemy (in a browser) uses Google Analytics to count visits. That does not apply to the iPhone app.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/mediavanity-dev/alchemy.
