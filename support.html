Alchemy Support

Sing, hum or play a melody, and Alchemy finds its key and the chords that fit.

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/mediavanity-dev/alchemy/issues. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were recording.

Alchemy doesn't hear me

Check that Alchemy has microphone access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone. For quiet singing, hold the phone about 30 cm (a foot) away and record somewhere without loud background noise.

How do I import a Voice Memo?

In Voice Memos, tap the recording, tap ⋯, then Share, and choose Alchemy. If Alchemy isn't listed, scroll to the end of the row of apps, tap More and add it. Any audio file in Files can also be opened with Import audio on the Capture screen.

How do I get my song into GarageBand or another DAW?

On the Harmony screen, tap the share button, then MIDI file. You get separate melody, chords and bass tracks, laid on the beat grid.

Where are my recordings stored?

Only on your iPhone. Alchemy has no accounts and sends nothing anywhere. Deleting the app deletes your saved takes, so export anything you want to keep first. See the privacy policy.
